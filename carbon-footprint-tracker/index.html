      Explore the model and emission factors

      Weekly commuting is converted using 52 ÷ 12 weeks per month. Kilometers are converted at 1.609344 km per mile. Diet uses 30 days; annual projections multiply the monthly estimate by 12. CO₂e means carbon dioxide equivalent.

      Uncertainty: high confidence uses ±10%, medium ±25%, low ±50%. Total lower and upper bounds are the sums of the category bounds. We use summed bounds, not quadrature: there is no evidence that factor errors are independent. These are assumed sensitivity bands, not statistical confidence intervals.

      Limitations: factors have differing system boundaries and remain unverified. Zero for walking and cycling covers direct operational emissions only. Flight effects, local electricity mix, food sourcing, waste composition and bag size are not modeled. Household results assume equal personal habits. The tool excludes purchases, public services and other emissions.
